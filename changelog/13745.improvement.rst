Fixture discovery now accepts subclasses of the internal ``FixtureFunctionDefinition`` type, allowing plugins to build on top of :func:`pytest.fixture`.

Fixtures hidden behind other decorators now emit a :class:`~pytest.PytestWarning` at their definition instead of silently going missing:

- a fixture wrapped by a custom decorator (e.g. using :func:`functools.wraps`) is not discovered;
- ``@classmethod`` above ``@pytest.fixture`` is not discovered -- place ``@pytest.fixture`` above ``@classmethod``;
- ``@staticmethod`` above ``@pytest.fixture`` is still discovered, but place ``@pytest.fixture`` above ``@staticmethod`` instead.
