Fixture discovery now accepts subclasses of ``FixtureFunctionDefinition``. Fixtures hidden behind other decorators (``functools.wraps``-style decorators or ``@classmethod`` placed above ``@pytest.fixture``) now emit a :class:`~pytest.PytestWarning` instead of being silently ignored. ``@staticmethod`` above ``@pytest.fixture`` still works but warns; place ``@pytest.fixture`` above ``@classmethod``/``@staticmethod`` instead.
